About the property

Silver Creek Log Cabin is a quiet frontier retreat set beside the river and surrounding wetlands. The timber residence includes a comfortable living area, separate kitchen and bedroom, with an enclosed yard and adjoining stable for horses.

The property is intended as a community build and can be downloaded and installed using the files supplied by its creator.
